# Receiver

Schematics: [stage 1 — sch2](../schematics/sch2-receiver-stage1.png) · [stage 4 — sch4](../schematics/sch4-receiver-node.png) (generated by [../schematics/render_schematics.py](../schematics/render_schematics.py))

- Stage 1 (measurements): Langevin transducer RX (both leads floating — do not ground!) → Schottky bridge (4×SS14) → RC filter (10k || 100n) → 5 V TVS → **47 kΩ in series** → ADS1115 A0 (the resistor limits current into the ADC's protection diodes: the TVS clamps ~9 V above the input's abs. max).
- Stage 2 (watts): RX → the same bridge → known resistive load (and/or LED), measure DC V and I after the bridge; power is V·I into that load. Protocol: [experiments/002](../../experiments/002-watts-3mm-steel/README.md).
- Stage 4 (node): RX → GY-LTC3588 **straight into PZ1/PZ2** (the bridge is built into the LTC3588-1, no external one needed) → 1 F supercapacitor → ESP32 (deep sleep + duty cycle). Load modulation — 2N7002 + 100 Ω on the **DC side** (the module's VIN pin, see sch4); a single MOSFET across the AC piezo does not work — the body diode shunts one half-wave (docs/03).

IMPORTANT: fit the TVS before the very first power-up — an open piezo at resonance puts out tens to hundreds of volts. On the DC side after the bridge — a unidirectional SMBJ5.0A; across the node's piezo (AC) — only a bidirectional SMBJ15CA.
